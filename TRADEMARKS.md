# Trademark policy

**Progressive Web Office**, **PWO** and the fanned-sheets logo (the files of
the [brand folder](https://github.com/progressive-web-office/progressive-web-office.github.io/tree/main/brand)) are the marks of the Progressive Web Office project, held by
Sébastien Celles. This policy says how they may be used.

The source code is free software under the GNU Affero General Public
License, version 3 (AGPL-3.0). That licence covers the code, not the marks:
as its section 7(e) allows, it grants no right to use the project's names or
logo. You may copy, change and redistribute the code under the AGPL; using
the marks is governed by this policy.

## Allowed without asking

- **Referring to the project** truthfully: "documents made with Progressive
  Web Office", "compatible with PWO", "a plugin for Progressive Web Office",
  articles, talks, teaching material, reviews, links.
- **Unmodified builds**: distributing or hosting the official application,
  built from an official release or commit **without changes** other than
  configuration (for example a school hosting it on its own server), under
  its own name and logo.
- **Plugins and content packs** may say they are "for Progressive Web
  Office", without using the logo as their own icon.
- Showing the logo **next to a link** to the project, at its original
  colours and proportions (see the [brand guide](https://github.com/progressive-web-office/progressive-web-office.github.io/blob/main/brand/README.md)).

## Needs written permission

- **Modified versions** (forks) must **not** be called Progressive Web
  Office or PWO and must not use the logo. Choose another name and icon; you
  may say "based on Progressive Web Office".
- Using the names or logo in a **product, service, company or domain name**,
  or in a way that suggests endorsement by, or affiliation with, the
  project.
- **Merchandise** (shirts, stickers…) beyond personal or community use
  without profit.

## Never

- Using the marks in a way that is misleading, or for something that could
  harm users (malware, phishing, data collection under the project's name).
- Changing the logo (colours, proportions, other sheets) and presenting it
  as the project's.

## Asking

Open an issue in
[progressive-web-office](https://github.com/progressive-web-office/progressive-web-office/issues)
or write to the maintainer. When in doubt, ask: permission is usually given
for community uses.

This policy may change; the version in the
[brand folder](https://github.com/progressive-web-office/progressive-web-office.github.io/blob/main/brand/TRADEMARKS.md)
of the organisation's site is the current one.
